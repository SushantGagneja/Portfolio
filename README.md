## About Me

Hey! I'm Sushant, a developer specializing in ML, AI & Robotics, with a deep interest in Quant Finance.

I'm very ADHD-pilled in everything I love, and I bring a lot of fun, weird, interesting ideas to life. I call them **SideQuests**, and the process of building them out is what I like to call **lore-maxxing**.

I nerd out about:

- Machine Learning & LLMs
- Quant Finance
- Inference infrastructure & architecture
- Hardware (FPGAs, microcontrollers)
- Robotics
- Mathematics, Physics, Astronomy
- Retro gaming

One recurring theme in my work: taking insanely complex systems, like Tesla's HydraNet or Palantir's Gotham, and building scaled-down versions of them just to understand how they tick.

## Currently Working On

-  SNN-BNN ultra-low latency inference on FPGAs, built for quant firms
-  A Macintosh from scratch, literally starting from logic gates
-  Prev: WorldQuant

Follow along to stay in the loop :p
